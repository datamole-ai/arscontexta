I work at Datamole, a data & AI consultancy (~80+ people, Prague/Brno) that delivers custom IoT, ML, and AI solutions primarily for agriculture, manufacturing, biotech, and foodtech clients. We've completed 100+ projects.

Our knowledge is structured in layers: Customer (e.g., Lely, Thermo Fisher, Agrifac) -> Umbrella (a broader engagement or product area within a customer) -> Project (a specific deliverable or workstream).

Within each project, I want to track:

- Data science experiments - hypotheses, datasets used, model approaches, metrics, results, and conclusions
- Meeting notes - decisions made, action items, attendees, follow-ups
- Specifications & requirements - functional/technical specs, constraints, acceptance criteria
- PRDs - product requirements documents, scope, goals, success metrics
- Technical decisions - architecture choices, trade-offs, ADRs
- Lessons learned - what worked, what didn't, reusable patterns
- Domain knowledge - industry/customer-specific context that accumulates over time
- Key contacts & stakeholders per customer

I also want cross-cutting views: patterns that transfer across customers/industries, reusable solution blueprints, and the ability to surface relevant past experiments or decisions when starting similar new work.

Make your best calls on everything - no follow-up questions needed, just set it up.
